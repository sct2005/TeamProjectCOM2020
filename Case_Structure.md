# AI Failure Exhibit – Case Structure

## 1. Exhibit Overview
- **Title**
- **Domain** (e.g. healthcare, flooding, urban planning)
- **Deployment Context** (where and how it was used)
- **Intended Use** (what the system was supposed to do)

## 2. AI System Description
- **System Type** (e.g. chatbot, classifier, decision-support tool)
- **Inputs & Assumptions**
- **Outputs Presented to Users**

## 3. Failure Description
- **What Went Wrong**
- **How the Failure Was Detected**
- **Who Was Affected**

## 4. Contributing Factors
- **Data Issues** (e.g. bias, missing data, poor quality)
- **Design or Technical Choices**
- **Organisational / Governance Factors**

## 5. Timeline of Failure
- **Key Events Leading to the Failure**
- **When the Failure Became Visible**
- **Aftermath or Response**

## 6. Supporting Artefacts
- **Artefact Type** (screenshot, diagram, report, summary)
- **Brief Description**
- **Source or Reference (if applicable)**

## 7. Lessons Learned
- **Key Lessons**
- **Practical Recommendations**
- **Warnings for Future Use**

## 8. Reflection & Learning Prompts
- **Key Assumption to Question**
- **Uncertainty or Bias Identified**
- **Appropriate vs Inappropriate Use**

## 9. Quiz (Optional)
- **Question**
- **Correct Answer**
- **Explanation**
